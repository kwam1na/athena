Problem and outcome

The first production weekly report used the wrong lifecycle evidence for scheduled-day closure and presented a sales-fold reconciliation as counted-cash variance. It also omitted tender composition and the product-level expense evidence managers need to understand operating consumption.

Outcome: Daily Close freezes complete evidence once; one weekly aggregator produces live, accepted, and corrected projections; Reports and email render the appropriate state without re-reading mutable source data.

Scope and boundaries

Included

  • Correct scheduled-close status and counted-cash variance.
  • Payment value/share first, tender-use count second.
  • Top five expense products by spend and by quantity, each with a remainder.
  • Live week-to-date, accepted history, Reports, and email.
  • Exact Aug 3–9 correction, production apply, Reports and HTML preview.

Explicitly excluded

  • No prior-week comparisons, categories, vendors, staff, or report-level detail.
  • No redesign of payment capture, expenses, or inventory consumption.
  • No approval tokens, repair-run tables, generalized migration framework, or correction history.
  • No corrected-send path and no corrected email until a later explicit decision.
  • No unrelated accepted-week repairs.

Key decisions

AreaDecision
Existing railsDaily Close remains the certification boundary; accepted projections remain immutable; correction uses one set-once amendment-style field; notification preparation remains on the existing registry rail.
Expense evidenceFreeze an independently versioned product sub-contract. Cap-plus-one reads, safe numeric checks, and exact spend reconciliation determine lane completeness without blocking the rest of Daily Close.
Weekly computationOne pure server-owned aggregator handles cash, payment, and expense evidence for live materialization, acceptance, repair, and verification. UI and email only format.
CoverageEach lane is complete, partial, or unavailable with its own usable/scheduled day count. Certified zero is covered; missing legacy evidence is not zero.
RankingSpend and quantity are independent deterministic top-five lists. Their unnumbered remainder is “1 other product” or “N other products.”
Accepted correctionPreserve the original baseline and fingerprint. Store one set-once correction projection with provenance; identical reruns no-op and a different correction rejects.
Email stateOriginal automatic intents always render the baseline. Explicit corrected preview matches corrected Reports and creates no notification or provider state.
Repair safetyA private internal command resolves the fixed store/week through indexed identifiers, verifies baseline and source fingerprints, re-derives on apply, and exposes no caller-selected target or committed production IDs.

Implementation units

Every feature-bearing unit begins with failing tests. Dependencies remain linear through evidence materialization, then split only where Reports and email can proceed independently.

U1

Certify expense-product evidence in Daily Close

Freeze bounded product identity, quantity, and spend. Empty is complete; overflow, invalid numbers, or reconciliation mismatch makes only this lane unavailable.

convex/operations/dailyClose.ts · schema · focused tests

U2

Aggregate shared weekly close evidence

Build the pure deterministic cash/payment/expense contract, per-lane coverage, value-first mix, both rankings, and stable remainders.

convex/reports/weeklyCloseEvidence.ts · focused tests

U3

Materialize live and accepted lifecycle states

Use resolved scheduled close lineage, preserve accepted baseline fields, and keep reopened current truth separate from historical truth.

convex/reports/weekly.ts · derived schema · lifecycle tests

U4

Project and render Reports

Keep reads source-free and bounded. Present variance facts distinctly, then payment mix and the two expense rankings with lane-specific coverage and “Report corrected” metadata.

U5

Render email and side-effect-free preview

Share contract and wording rules, make baseline retry selection explicit, and render exact corrected subject/HTML without intent, schedule, delivery, or provider effects.

Dry-run exact retained evidence, compare canonical fingerprints, apply one idempotent correction field, and expose corrected Reports and HTML for review.

Merge, deploy, and verify

Rebuild generated artifacts, clear the authoritative merge gate and reviewer loop, merge, deploy Convex and web, apply the exact repair, hand back the preview, and stop before send.

Primary risks and controls

Silent truncation

Cap-plus-one reads and explicit completeness; never publish a truncated ranking.

False zero

Absent legacy evidence renders unavailable or partial, while certified zero remains covered.

Historical mutation

Original accepted fields remain byte-stable; correction is orthogonal and set once.

Wrong email content

Pending original intents stay baseline-only; correction preview is explicit and side-effect free.

Repair drift

Exact bounded source manifest, baseline/candidate fingerprints, per-day reconciliation, and re-derivation on apply.

Surface drift

One contract shape and shared wording rules; state-specific Reports/email parity tests.

Verification and finish line

  1. Write failing focused tests before each behavioral change, then make them pass.
  2. Verify persisted Daily Close, weekly lifecycle, projection, UI, email, repair, and notification boundaries.
  3. Rebuild deterministic Graphify and generated artifacts; run bun run pr:athena on the final staged tree.
  4. Resolve every gate and reviewer finding; require unanimous correctness, integrity, security/reliability, testing, product, and design review.
  5. Merge the PR, align root, deploy Convex and Athena web, and verify the merged revision in production.
  6. Dry-run and apply only Wigclub Aug 3–9; confirm six-of-six closure, cash variance, payment mix, product rankings, and exact reconciliation.
  7. Return corrected Reports and exact email HTML to the user, with proof that no corrected email was sent.
Hard stop: corrected delivery remains outside this plan. The user reviews and tweaks the rendered report before any later send decision.
