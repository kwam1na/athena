Schedule-aware end-of-week reporting

A close-aware Weekly tab for full administrators, built on Athena’s deterministic report-day model. Store Schedule defines the cycle and operational dates; hours never filter financial activity. Accepted values remain fixed while later truth appears as an amendment, and the current Overview remains unchanged.

Product contract

The schedule decides dates, not which transactions count

Cycle and membership

  • Store Schedule owns an effective-dated cycle-start weekday, default Monday.
  • weeklyClosedDays and date-exception closed flags determine operational dates.
  • Opening, closing, split, and cross-midnight windows never filter financial activity.
  • No weekday—including Sunday—has hard-coded special behavior.

Lifecycle

  • Live WTD becomes eligible for acceptance after the final scheduled date’s Daily Close.
  • Close posture and amendment posture are independent.
  • The accepted baseline is immutable; current truth can change.
  • Historical accepted reports begin after feature launch—no retrospective invention.
Outside-schedule activity: it is excluded from headline totals, disclosed within its original seven-date frame, and never moved into an adjacent reporting week.
Workspace placement: Weekly is a sibling tab between Overview and Items. Overview remains the default, retains its existing controls and queries, and restores its validated period state after a Weekly round trip.
Technical shape

Extend the current Reports model—do not build a second one

Accepted truth

The close acceptance timestamp is the knowledge cutoff. Server-only observedAt is indexed by store and operating date. The baseline folds all seven frame dates at or before the cutoff, then partitions headline and outside-schedule values.

Current truth

Bounded reportDay composition feeds one live Weekly singleton. One current-amendment projection compares latest truth with the accepted baseline; Overview starts no Weekly query.

Close isolation

EOW adds no new Daily Close transaction write. The sweeper derives intent from the existing close fact and dirty day, with bounded reconciliation for a missing intent.

Inventory evidence

Accepted attention comes from the final close’s frozen logical-work snapshot. Mutable Open Work is used only for live/current posture and investigation routes.

Delivery map

Eight dependency-ordered implementation units

U1

Schedule anchor and weekly resolver

Widen the schedule contract, stage anchor changes safely, resolve effective membership, comparisons, zero dates, and outside-schedule dates.

U2

Aggregate payment posture

Characterize emitters; normalize collection, payment refunds, allocation, unsettled and coverage; introduce version-aware fingerprints.

U3

Weekly projections and accepted baseline

Add observedAt, cutoff indexes, one bounded atomic seven-frame fold, immutable accepted values, and current amendment posture. A failed build leaves the prior verified projection unchanged.

U4

Close, reopen, and late-fact lifecycle

Derive intent from existing close evidence, reconcile missed intents, and keep close and amendment postures orthogonal.

U5

Grouped inventory attention

Reuse logical Open Work grouping, freeze accepted posture from final-close evidence, and classify new versus carried-forward once per group.

U6

Protected briefing and history reads

Expose active/selected briefing, paginated history, and historical detail through bounded, full-admin, store-scoped contracts.

U7

Additive Reports Weekly tab

Add the sibling route and tab, preserve Overview state, render the five operator questions, and verify the Apple-informed hierarchy, responsive behavior, accessibility, URL-stable history, and owner routes.

U8

Migration, verification, and rollout

Backfill anchor and observation time, add projection-only repair, verify from sources, and prove exactness, isolation, pagination, and two-second p95 reads on one representative non-empty 10× fixture before rollout.

Release proof

Source reconciliation and operator comprehension both gate rollout

Independent source verification

  • Schedule membership and outside-schedule partition.
  • Net/gross sales, recognized sales refunds, units, cost coverage.
  • Collection, payment refunds, eligible allocation, coverage, invalid over-allocation, and unsettled.
  • Close lineage, amendment deltas, and logical inventory groups.

Operator acceptance

  • Representative full administrators review live, accepted, amended/reopened, and incomplete weeks.
  • They answer all five weekly questions without cross-workspace arithmetic.
  • Unresolved ambiguity or continued manual calculation blocks wider rollout.
  • Overview remains the default and unchanged beyond the shared Weekly tab.

Apple-design UI evidence

  • Desktop and compact layouts are reviewed functionally; no screenshots are required.
  • Keyboard order, touch targets, 200% zoom, and horizontal-overflow checks.
  • Reduced-motion, reduced-transparency, and increased-contrast checks.
  • Vitest proves semantics, tab-state restoration, and that Overview starts no Weekly query.

Database-read proof

  • Instrumented counts match each handler and builder’s documented read budget.
  • Weekly uses no operational-source subscription and never exceeds two projection-backed subscriptions.
  • A representative non-empty 10× fixture remains exact, store-isolated, paginated, and within the two-second p95 public-read target.
  • If it cannot fit one bounded atomic build safely, rollout stays disabled pending a focused plan revision.
Activation remains store-scoped until anchor and observedAt migrations pass, source verification has zero unexplained mismatches, read caps are clean, and operator acceptance succeeds.
Scope discipline

Explicitly not part of V1

Deferred

  • Organization-wide and cross-store reporting.
  • Manager or pos_only access.
  • Payment-method mix and method-level fact expansion.
  • Generic low-stock, velocity, days-cover, forecasting, or configurable thresholds.
  • Email/notification delivery and retrospective accepted EOW history.

Outside the product

  • Accounting profit, bank reconciliation, payroll, tax, rent, utilities, or cash flow.
  • Mutating payments, closes, stock, register sessions, or Open Work from Reports.
  • Moving outside-schedule activity into another week.
  • Using operating hours as a financial eligibility gate.
