Executive summary

V26-1413 gives later extraction work a reviewed source of truth for Athena's current delivery workflow. It records 14 normative sources, 34 assertions (31 source-backed, 2 explicitly approved, and 1 observed-only), 24 bounded members, 84 direct dependencies, 18 classifications, 13 reference dispositions, 7 representative scenarios, and 145 deliberately out-of-scope files.

The delivery is characterization only. It does not move skills, change discovery roots, implement a tracker adapter, or replace Athena's repository-specific gates.

Why this baseline was needed

Athena's reusable delivery behavior was spread across skill bodies, repository policy, references, and harness obligations. Extracting from that moving surface without an approved boundary could silently omit required behavior or export Athena-only rules as portable policy.

A structural inventory alone was insufficient. Two records could be edited together and remain internally consistent while changing the meaning later tickets would treat as approved.

Mental model: a certified survey map

Think of the baseline as a survey completed before construction. The JSON artifacts mark the approved boundaries. The checker compares those boundaries with the current repository. The fixtures prove representative routes through the map.

Workflow rules may be portable candidate, optional adapter, retained Athena overlay, or excluded. Bounded closure members use the narrower U11 taxonomy of portable candidate, retained overlay, or excluded. These labels guide later work; they are not migration actions in this ticket.

Before and after

Before: an agent had to reconstruct the portable boundary from many live files, and coherent edits could change semantics without a single parity sensor noticing.

After: committed artifacts describe the approved boundary, small declarative contracts pin exact meaning, mutation tests demonstrate known false-green cases, and one read-only command reports drift without rewriting files.

What changed and what did not

  • Added a versioned characterization baseline and overlay map with exact classifications, bounded closure, dependencies, and residual inventory.
  • Added a read-only portable-baseline checker, strict document validation, filesystem containment helpers, and exact semantic contracts.
  • Added 7 representative fixtures and mutation-heavy focused coverage for malformed, missing, rebound, broadened, and filesystem-drift cases.
  • Moved the approved brainstorm and Markdown/HTML plan into the delivery branch so the implementation evidence travels with the ticket.
  • Captured the reusable lesson that authoritative characterization must bind exact meaning while rejecting review-driven scope growth.
  • Did not modify .agents/skills, create .claude/skills, change active discovery, extract portable skills, implement Linear behavior, or replace Athena's merge, telemetry, reporting, and deployment overlays.

Failure boundaries

  • Schema-invalid or unexpectedly shaped JSON fails validation instead of being trusted through unchecked casts.
  • Missing, noncanonical, escaping, or prohibited symlink identities fail the bounded characterization checks.
  • Schema-valid rebinding of sources, members, rules, dependencies, citations, notes, or scenarios fails exact semantic comparison.
  • Residual discovery drift, including executable-mode and symlink changes, changes the certified tree identity.
  • The checker intentionally does not become a universal filesystem validator; a proposed hardening idea was excluded when it could not demonstrate a concrete V26-1413 outcome failure.
