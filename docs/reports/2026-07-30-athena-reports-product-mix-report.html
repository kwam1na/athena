Executive summary

Operator outcome

The Overview keeps the full Days table visible while a selected day scopes only Products sold. Items adds period-wide units, transactions, range disclosure, and product economics.

System outcome

Shared state-motion primitives coordinate populated and empty states. Backend totals no longer depend on the current SKU page, and bounded reads refuse to return partial transaction counts.

Why this mattered

The earlier interaction treated “inspect this day” as “replace the report range.” That destroyed table context, reset pagination, and caused flashes as data and page state settled. Motion fixes applied to one child at a time also left sibling totals or legends disappearing before the outgoing surface had finished.

Core model: range is the operator's working context; selected day is a temporary lens over the product mix. They must not be the same piece of state.

Mental model

Working range

Owns Days rows, pagination, date-range label, and the remembered page.

Selection lens

selectedDay scopes only the product mix. Selecting the same row again clears it.

Data state owner

Totals, freshness, and results leave together as one keyed populated state.

Stable surface boundary

Intrinsic height retargets smoothly; responsive chart measurement stays mounted.

Failure boundaries and residual risk

  • Backend must deploy first. The frontend consumes additive totals and SKU cost fields. Older clients safely ignore the additions.
  • Unclosed-day reads are bounded. More than 2,000 report facts for one day fails the Items query instead of reporting a partial transaction count.
  • Closed-day totals trust the linked summary. A missing or invalid summary.transactionCount resolves to 0 rather than falling back to live POS facts.
  • Month fan-out is the main runtime risk. A range containing many unclosed, high-volume days can perform substantial bounded reads.
  • Reporting remains asynchronous. Period totals and SKU rollups may briefly disagree while independent reporting materializations settle.
  • Margin is current-catalog context. It is net price minus current unit cost, not historical realized margin for each sale.
  • Browser motion remains worth observing. Recharts, ResizeObserver, IntersectionObserver, Framer Motion, and Anime.js meet at this boundary.

Intentionally unchanged

  • Revenue/units ranking, cursor pagination, table metrics, and SKU drill-down links.
  • Newest-first Days ordering and 14-row pagination.
  • The explicit date link still opens the day Items drill-down.
  • The mix remains the top five SKUs plus an “Other SKUs” aggregate.
  • Shared tables resize instantly by default; smooth resize is opt-in.
  • Reduced-motion paths avoid spatial animation or use immediate/fade-only changes.
