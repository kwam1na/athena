What is being delivered

Daily Close and historical EOD snapshots include a store's whole local calendar day, including after-hours sales. Ask Athena gets quieter initial suggestions and remembers which committed answers have already appeared, so reopening the panel does not replay their animation.

The two original PRs are rebased together on current main. The requested finish line is one merged PR, local root alignment, and verified Convex and Athena webapp production deployments. Those events are pending in this candidate report.

Why the changes matter

Scheduled opening and closing hours describe when a shop expects to trade. They do not contain every valid sale. Using those hours as the snapshot boundary can omit a sale recorded after closing. A UTC day can likewise split a non-UTC store's local date.

The visual panel can disappear and return while its shell stays mounted. If animation history lives only in the panel, a replayed subscription snapshot can make an old answer look newly delivered.

Two boundaries with different lifetimes

Use trading hours to decide when automation should act. Use the local calendar day to decide which timestamped records the snapshot contains. Operating-date stamps keep unsettled drawers associated with their day; settled closeout evidence and unstamped legacy drawers still require clock-based attribution.

An answer's arrival belongs to the persistent shell. The panel consumes that decision and paints a seen answer fully when reopened.

Before and after

  • Daily Close without explicit bounds: UTC day becomes the governing schedule's local calendar day. Missing or invalid schedules retain the existing UTC fallback.
  • Historical EOD: trading-window snapshot bounds become calendar-day bounds. Trading hours and schedule evidence remain the timing authority; unresolved ranges quarantine.
  • Unsettled stamped drawers: the final attribution pass now preserves them outside explicit snapshot hours. Future unstamped drawers remain excluded.
  • Suggested questions: a centered disclosure opens on hover and can be pinned with a click. The empty neutral status is removed.
  • Answer reveal: the persistent shell records turn ID and commit time, preventing replay after collapse and reopen.

Scope and limits

The delivery changes the schedule helper, Daily Close, historical EOD automation, the agent panel, their tests, the store-day read-port compatibility version, and generated artifacts. It adds no schema, public API route, authorization capability, or inventory write path.

Explicit valid caller bounds still take precedence. Reduced motion remains respected. The change does not backfill or rewrite completed Daily Close snapshots, and the local tests do not prove historical production data has been repaired.

Extending these paths later

See the Daily Close store-day lesson for the reusable implementation guidance.

  • Choose a range from the consumer's purpose: automation timing and record inclusion are distinct.
  • Exercise the final snapshot, since a later filter can undo a correct source-query change.
  • Make legacy fixture ordering match Convex's undefined-before-string ordering.
  • Test persistent-shell behavior through the shell, rather than supplying a reveal override directly to the panel.
  • Transitive snapshot changes can change agent read-port behavior. Bump both compatibility declarations, regenerate, fence before deploy, smoke with the profile disabled, and restore its prior enabled state.
  • Deploy Convex and the Athena webapp after merge; the in-app documentation corpus is part of the webapp bundle.
